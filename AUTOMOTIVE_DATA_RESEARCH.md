# Automotive Data Research Prompt

Research the requested automotive information for the exact vehicle configuration.

Determine applicability before selecting a value. Prefer factory/manufacturer documentation. Record source, publisher, document/version, section/page, retrieval date, applicability, reviewer, verification state, and conflicts.

Distinguish verified specification, general guidance, user observation, diagnostic hypothesis, derived value, and unknown.

If authoritative evidence is unavailable, return SOURCE_REQUIRED or UNKNOWN rather than guessing.
